Remember — GPIO 6–11 touch the SPI flash: avoid them. GPIO18/34/35/36/39 are input-only (that's fine for ECHO). Keep GPIO16/17 free for the optional NEO-6M GPS (TX→16, RX←17, 5V→divider into 16). If the car drives the wrong way, don't rewire — flip MOTOR_L_INV / MOTOR_R_INV in the code.

Why the relay goes here: the firmware's force-stop command sets GPIO23 LOW, which opens the relay and cuts the battery rail to the L298N — motors die instantly even if the ESP32 is bricked. The old diagram's "relay → buzzers" chain is removed — the buzzers get the 5 V bus and GPIO4 directly.

Before connecting the ESP32

1 · Power orderPower the buck converter ALONE and confirm a multimeter reads 5.00 V at OUT. Only then connect the ESP32, relay and sensors.
2 · No dual supplyNever have USB and the buck 5 V on the ESP32 at the same time — unplug the VIN wire before flashing over USB.
3 · Cell polaritySeries joint: cell A (+) → cell B (−). Pack + = cell B (+), pack − = cell A (−). Check twice.
4 · First boot checkSerial Monitor (115200) should show "WiFi OK", one LED on, and a heartbeat every 250 ms. Buzzer silent, relay silent at rest.
